About TIET. Learn more about us.

Welcome to Talent Identification & Empowerment Trust (TIET). Our organization aims to empower students and individuals through education, technology and skill development.

We encourage learning, innovation and personal growth. Our goal is to create opportunities for people to develop their skills and build a better future.

Our Mission

To envision a society with educated, employed and committed youth who work towards the growth and prosperity of the community. To guide and motivate young people towards acquiring quality education, developing professional skills and building successful careers for a bright future. To inspire them to dream big, think beyond boundaries and explore new opportunities. We aim to develop leadership qualities, self-confidence and positive attitudes among youth. To encourage them to work hard, think smart, overcome challenges and achieve higher goals. Our mission is to empower every young individual to become responsible citizens and contribute to creating a better society.

Our History

Talent Identification & Empowerment Trust (TIET) saw its dawn in 2011 with a vision to empower individuals through quality education, skill development, and social responsibility. The organisation was established with the enthusiasm and commitment of its founder members, who dreamed of creating meaningful opportunities and building a brighter future for underprivileged and deserving students.

Along with academic support, TIET focuses on skill-based training programmes, including computer skills, communication skills, coding, and Tally. The organisation also provides exposure to higher learning through seminars, workshops, awareness programmes, and career guidance activities.

Our commitment extends beyond education. We continuously explore innovative, need-based projects that promote personal growth, professional development, social awareness, and community welfare. Through these efforts, we strive to create opportunities that help individuals become confident, skilled, and responsible members of society.

Today, TIET continues to plan, develop, and execute impactful initiatives in alignment with its mission and vision. With dedication, teamwork, and a strong belief in the power of education, we remain committed to transforming lives and building a better tomorrow.

Our Library

Talent Identification & Empowerment Trust (TIET) provides a free library facility for students to improve their knowledge and learning skills.

Our library offers free books, educational materials, history books, science books, computer books, general knowledge, storybooks and competitive exam preparation materials.

Students can access free books and learning resources to develop reading habits, gain knowledge and build a brighter future.

Our Library Facilities

  • Science and Computer books
  • History and General Knowledge books
  • Storybooks and educational materials
  • Free books for all students
  • Competitive exam preparation books
  • Free learning resources
